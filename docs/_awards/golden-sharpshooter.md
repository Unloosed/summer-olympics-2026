---
title: Golden Sharpshooter
description: Awarded to the participant with the highest ratio of Gold medals to total events participated in.
icon: 🎯
criteria: Highest Gold medal / participation ratio. Must have participated in a minimum of 50% of events (22).
---

The **Golden Sharpshooter** recognizes the competitor who made the most of their opportunities. Rather than rewarding sheer volume, it honors participants who turned a high percentage of their appearances into Gold-medal finishes.
