  Bocce is a ball sport belonging to the boules family, closely related to British bowls and French pétanque. The goal is to get your bocce balls closer to the "pallino" than your opponent.
ruleset: |
  1. A game starts with a coin toss to decide who throws the pallino.
  2. Players take turns rolling or throwing their balls toward the pallino.
  3. Points are awarded for each ball that is closer to the pallino than the opponent's closest ball.
  4. Games are typically played to 12 or 16 points.
